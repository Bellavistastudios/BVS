Frequently asked questions.

How does the collaboration work?

After an initial conversation, we shape the concept together in a joint session, then take care of filming, editing, and delivery ourselves, so you always know what to expect at every step.

How much effort does this take on your end?

Very little. Depending on the project, you might join a short kickoff session to align on the idea, but filming, editing, and publishing are entirely on us. No in-house camera or editing skills required.

Do you need a briefing from us, or do you develop the ideas yourselves?

Both. We start with your goals and brand, then develop the concept and script ourselves together with you in a short session, so the idea fits your business without you having to write a full creative brief.

Can you also work with existing material, like old footage or photos?

Yes. We regularly draw on existing archives, footage from past shoots, and previous campaigns, re-cutting, re-grading, and refining them to match today's content standards instead of starting from scratch.

Do you also publish the content on our channels, or just deliver the files?

Both options work. For ongoing partnerships, we typically publish and manage the content directly on your channels as part of the retainer. If you'd rather handle publishing yourselves, we simply deliver the final files.
